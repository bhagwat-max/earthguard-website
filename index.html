BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

A few good
questions.

What should I send for a quotation?+

Share your bill of materials or a list with product names, specifications, quantities and delivery location. Mention any drawings or datasheets when you contact us.

Can I enquire about a small quantity?+

Yes. Send your requirement and we’ll check sourcing options. Minimum quantities, pricing and delivery terms depend on the product and supplier.

Do you supply materials for solar projects?+

We welcome enquiries for solar earthing, bonding and protection materials. Send your project’s approved BOM so the required products can be checked.

Do you provide design or installation?+

Our current focus is material sourcing and supply. Qualified electrical professionals should handle system design, installation and testing.
